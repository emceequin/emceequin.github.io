Authorized use

VFX Package Organizer is a privately shared macOS utility provided by its individual developer. This informational website does not provide an app download or grant rights to third-party material.

Review your files and results

You are responsible for selecting appropriate folders, checking organization results, and maintaining independent backups. Review warnings, duplicates, recovery status, and Undo results before relying on a run. While the app is organizing, leave the selected server, IN, and SHOTTREE unchanged. Don’t move, rename, replace, or delete anything there until the operation finishes. The app is not a backup service.
