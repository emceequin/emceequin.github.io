A practical workflow

Selected folders.
Recorded results.

The app works with folders you select, which may be on your Mac or on a mounted network location. It copies supported media into an organized destination and records run details locally. Review results before relying on an organized package, and keep independent backups.

While the app is organizing, leave the selected server, IN, and SHOTTREE unchanged. Don’t move, rename, replace, or delete anything there until the operation finishes.

History provides a record of runs, including outcomes and file details. Recovery and Undo features use recorded run information; they are not a replacement for backups or checking your files.

Gmail is optional

A report when you want one.

Connect your account

Google Sign-In connects a Gmail sender and asks for permission to send email. File organization can be used without Gmail.

Enable reports

Reports go to the connected account by default. They can include package names, filenames, folder paths, results, and errors.

Stay in control

Disable reporting or disconnect locally. Removing the Google grant is a separate step in your Google Account.

Reports are sent through Google’s Gmail API while the app is running and reporting is enabled. Queued reports can be sent later. Review recipients and content carefully, especially for confidential production work. How reports and recipients work.
