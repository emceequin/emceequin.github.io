About this policy

This policy describes VFX Package Organizer’s current macOS Gmail integration and this informational website. The app is privately shared and supported by its individual developer. Contact vfxmacapptesting@gmail.com with privacy or support questions.

File organization does not require connecting a Google Account. Google authorization and sending reports are optional.

Google permissions and account information

The app requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send to send run reports from your connected Gmail account. Google classifies this as a sensitive permission. The app does not request inbox-reading, mailbox-modification, or mail-deletion permissions, and its Gmail implementation does not read your existing messages, contacts, or attachments.

Google Sign-In also requests basic sign-in identity and account-profile access (OpenID, email, and profile). The SDK can receive your email address, Google account identifier, name, and profile-picture information. The app uses the email address and account identifier to identify the connected sender; it displays the address and uses it for sender checks and the default report recipient. The app does not use your name or picture for its reporting feature.

Google handles authentication. The app does not ask you to give the developer your Google password. Google and your account administrator may apply their own authorization, security, and access policies.

Token storage and connections

The Google Sign-In SDK stores authorization state, including tokens, in macOS Keychain. The SDK can restore the connection and refresh tokens when needed, including after reopening the app. Disabling report sending does not by itself disconnect the account or prevent restoration.

Access tokens are used with HTTPS requests to Google’s Gmail API. Run records and email-queue JSON files contain report and diagnostic information, not the SDK’s OAuth tokens. Keychain storage is separate from the app’s local preferences and run records; it is not a promise that all local data is encrypted by the app.

The app sends authorization requests to Google and sends enabled reports through Google. It has no implemented developer-operated report collection or storage backend. Files in selected network or cloud-synced folders may also be processed by the services you choose for those folders.

Report contents, recipients, and delivery

A report is a plain-text email based on recorded run details. Its subject identifies VFX Package Organizer, the outcome, package name, and package type. The body can include counts, filenames, source and destination folder paths, duplicate details, warnings, errors, and recovery or resolution information. An event identifier is included in an email header. Media files are not attached by the report implementation.

When you connect, the saved report recipient is set to that account’s address. Reporting is enabled only when the connected sender matches that saved recipient. Each queued report keeps its recorded sender and recipient. The app provides explicit confirmation controls to reassign queued senders or recipients; a queued recipient can differ from the currently connected account. Switching accounts does not silently rewrite old queued recipients.

Google receives the sender, recipient, message contents, and authorization needed to send the report. The destination mailbox and its provider receive the email. The developer’s support address is not automatically copied on reports. Recipients can retain or forward messages under their own practices.

The app sends while it is running with reporting enabled, and may send eligible queued reports after a later launch or reconnection. Some temporary failures are retried automatically. An uncertain delivery requires attention rather than an automatic retry. Disabling reports or disconnecting stops further eligible sends but cannot recall a request already sent to Google or email already delivered.

Local records and retention

The app retains local preferences, selected-folder bookmarks, run History and journals, and an email queue in the Mac user’s app data. These can include file paths, filenames, timestamps, recorded sender and recipient addresses, report text, delivery attempts, errors, and Gmail message identifiers. The saved recipient remains in local preferences after disconnecting.

The current implementation has no automatic age-based retention or deletion schedule for History or queue records, including sent entries. Disconnecting does not delete these records, media, or delivered email. Removing the app alone should not be treated as a verified way to remove all app data, Keychain items, exports, or backups.

For help removing local records, contact support. Keep needed backups and resolve any active or recoverable file operation before manually removing app data. Data in exports, backups, selected storage services, Google, or recipient mailboxes must be managed separately; removing a local copy does not erase those copies.

Disconnecting and revoking Google access

Local disconnect: the app’s Disconnect action calls Google Sign-In’s local sign-out, clears its local Keychain authorization state, and disables report sending. It leaves queued reports, History, and the saved recipient in place. It does not call Google’s grant-revocation operation.

Google grant revocation: visit your Google Account’s third-party connections, select VFX Package Organizer, and remove its access or connection using Google’s controls. This is separate from disconnecting inside the app. It does not delete local records or recall sent messages. Reconnecting and granting permission again can allow reporting to resume when you enable it.

Google API data and Limited Use

VFX Package Organizer’s use and transfer of Google API data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, and the applicable Google Workspace user data and developer policy.

The integration uses Google data to connect your account and provide the visible email-reporting feature. It has no functionality to sell Google data, use it for advertising, build unrelated profiles, or train generalized AI models. It does not automatically provide the developer with access to your Google Account or mailbox.

If you choose to send Google-derived information to support, send only the specific information you want reviewed. Support correspondence is handled as described below. Google data must not be used or shared beyond the disclosed feature and the uses permitted by Google’s policies.

Support correspondence

Only the individual developer handles email sent to vfxmacapptesting@gmail.com. If you contact support, the developer receives your email address and whatever information you include and uses the correspondence to respond to your request. Gmail processes that correspondence as the email service provider.

There is currently no defined retention or deletion schedule for support email. No specific retention period is promised. You may ask the developer about deleting support correspondence; any request needs to be considered separately from local app records and copies retained in your own mailbox or backups. Do not send passwords, OAuth tokens, or confidential production material.

This informational website

These static pages add no analytics, tracking scripts, cookies, login, forms, backend, or embedded third-party content. Page styles and assets are served locally with the site. External links and the email contact open services with their own privacy practices.

When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. That hosting-level processing is separate from tracking added by this site. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes and questions

This policy should be updated if the app’s behavior or support practices change. Materially different uses of Google data require updated disclosures and any consent Google requires before that new use. Questions may be sent to vfxmacapptesting@gmail.com.
